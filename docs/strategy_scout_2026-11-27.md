What made the difference where something beat random or TPE: a prior that decays (piBO) or a search that falls back (MetaBO, RGPE), never a frozen preference; training data from many tasks (ZeroShotOpt, OptFormer), not one table; hybrids with the LLM as an operator inside a classical loop (CALM, EvoTune, Centaur, SLLMBO) with a reward relative to the incumbent and a diversity-preserving regulariser; and, in GRPO, no std normalisation, no all-tied groups, and reward for rare correct proposals. Our E4 has none of these and all three documented failure ingredients: one source table, a frozen policy, reverse-KL GRPO with std normalisation on a near-tied reward.
